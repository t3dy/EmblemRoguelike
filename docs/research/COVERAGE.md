# Corpus Coverage — every text gone through

This file records the **exhaustive pass** over the alchemy scholarship / primary-source
corpus at `E:\pdf\alchemy\Markdown` (96 work-folders). Each work below was mined or
catalogued by one of six parallel research agents. Status legend:

- **mined** — read substantively; specific game ideas extracted (see artifact links).
- **sampled** — large file(s) read by targeted search; high-yield passages harvested.
- **catalogued** — structure/ToC confirmed; themes already saturated by a deeper-read
  sibling, so no *new* distinct game material beyond what is cited elsewhere.
- **no-material** — read/checked but yields nothing usable (OCR failure, pure
  methodology/historiography).

All extracted ideas are **original game-design synthesis**, inspired by the sources;
authors/works are cited as pointers only (no copied passages — the texts are
copyrighted scholarship). New ideas from this pass are folded into the artifact files
([monsters](monsters.md) · [equipment](equipment.md) · [materials/operations](materials_operations.md) ·
[quests](quests.md) · [character creation](character_creation.md) · [patrons](patrons.md) ·
[locations](locations.md) · [events/status](events_status.md)) and summarised in
[NEW_IDEAS.md](NEW_IDEAS.md).
