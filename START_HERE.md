## How to Play

### Basic Controls
- **Arrow Keys / WASD** — Move
- **Z or Space** — Confirm / interact
- **X** — Cancel / save game
- **M** — Toggle music

### Gameplay Loop

1. **Create a Character** (Choose a class: Knight, Alchemist, Atalanta, Hippomenes)
2. **Explore the Overworld** and find NPCs
3. **Accept Emblem Quests** (King & Queen offer quests based on Maier's emblems)
4. **Gather Materials** (Mine vitriol, sulfur; collect charcoal)
5. **Enter a Castle** (Explore lab spaces)
6. **Perform Operations** (Use furnace to transform materials):
   - Set target temperature
   - Add materials
   - Wait for operation to complete (30-120 minutes)
   - Handle dangers if they occur
   - Receive yields + XP
7. **Progress** (Advance through emblem phases)
8. **Final Goal** (Achieve Philosopher's Stone OR other victory condition)

### Example Quest Path

**Emblem III: "Go to the Woman Who Washes"**
1. Accept quest from Queen
2. Gather 3 Vitriol + 2 Charcoal
3. Go to Furnace Chamber
4. Start "Dissolution" operation
5. Set furnace to Moderate temp (80-140°C)
6. Wait ~40 minutes, managing temperature
7. Danger: Vitriol fumes! Choose: abort (lose materials) or continue (take HP damage)
8. Operation completes
9. Receive: White Stone (material), 20 XP, 30 gold
10. Proceed to next emblem

## What Makes This Special

### 🎓 Grounded in History
All materials, operations, and dangers based on real alchemical practice:
- Vitriol from trade routes (Khunrath's brother's business)
- Mercury toxicity (historical furnace worker deaths)
- Sulfur explosions (real operational hazards)
- Furnace management (actual alchemical apparatus design)

### 🎯 Multiple Solution Paths
Each emblem has 2-3 quest variants:
- Perform the operation directly (operational quest)
- Broker peace between rival alchemists (diplomatic quest)
- Study the text and gain knowledge (scholarly quest)
- Tend a garden and harvest materials (agricultural quest)

### ⚠️ Real Consequences
- Failed operations lose expensive materials (no reset button)
- Dangerous furnace management has actual risk (temperature variance → instability)
- Material scarcity creates tension (can't spam operations infinitely)
- Patronage can be lost (poor results → exile from court)

### 🎮 Roguelike Permadeath Integration
- Players rarely complete all 50 emblems in one run (takes hours per run)
- Skill carries between runs (learned recipes, materials discovered)
- Each attempt pushes slightly further (9 emblems in run 1, 15 in run 2, 22 in run 3, etc.)
- Procedural world generation ensures variety

### 📖 Narrative & Mechanical
- Emblem mottos are BOTH poetic quest hooks AND mechanical puzzles
  - "Go to the woman who washes sheets" = Dissolution operation
  - "Make a circle of man and woman" = Conjunction of White Stone + Red Tincture
  - "The Stone lives in the fire" = Extreme-heat resistance test
- Completing emblem unlocks its full "Discourse" (scholarly explanation)
