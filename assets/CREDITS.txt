# Art credits

Pixel-art terrain decor and buildings (t_forest, t_forest2, s_town, s_house,
s_castle, s_door, s_church, s_furnace) are derived from Kenney asset packs,
all released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0, public domain):

- Tiny Town  — https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town
- Roguelike/RPG pack (forge) — https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack

All other sprites are extracted from public-domain alchemical emblem engravings
(Stolcius, Maier, et al.).
